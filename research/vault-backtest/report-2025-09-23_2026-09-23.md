# Vault APY bands vs the trailing 12 months — Tier A

**Window:** 2025-09-23 → 2026-09-23 · **Data:** Binance XRPUSDT 1-minute bars
(525,601) and perp funding (1,096 periods), FRED SOFR + 100 bps as the borrow
proxy · **Notional:** $100k per vault · **Tool:** `tools/vault_backtest/tier_a.py`

The UI advertises **MM 12–18 %**, **Delta-Neutral 15–25 %**, **Delta One 20–35 %**.
Nothing had ever tested those numbers. This is Tier A of
`docs/vault-backtest-plan.md`: a closed-form pass over real data, not a backtest
of shipped infrastructure.

---

## 1. Delta One's advertised band is contradicted by this window

This one needs no fill model, no synthetic flow and no assumption about our
order book. The strategy is funding carry on levered notional, less borrow on
the levered part. Both terms are measured.

    realised funding carry, whole window : +0.19 %
    per 8h: mean +0.017 bp, median +0.08 bp, q95 +1.00 bp
    borrow proxy (SOFR + 100 bp), mean   :  4.74 % APR

| leverage | realised APY | funding needed for the low end (20 %) |
|---|---|---|
| 1.5× | **−2.13 %** | 17.5 % annual = 1.60 bp per 8h, sustained |
| 2.0× | **−4.42 %** | 14.6 % annual = 1.33 bp per 8h, sustained |
| 3.0× | **−9.01 %** | 11.6 % annual = 1.06 bp per 8h, sustained |

The sign is wrong, not just the magnitude, and leverage makes it worse because
borrow outruns a carry that was not there.

**What the band assumes.** To reach even 20 % at 2× the funding rate would have
to hold **1.33 bp per 8h every period for a year**. The measured median was
**0.08 bp** and the **95th percentile was 1.00 bp** — so the pitch needs funding
above its own 95th percentile, continuously, for twelve months.

This is a statement about *this window*, not about the strategy forever: funding
was flat and symmetric (54 % of periods positive, q05 −1.37 bp, q95 +1.00 bp).
A sustained bull-basis regime would change it. But the advertised band currently
describes a market condition that did not occur.

## 2. The Delta-Neutral premium has no source in this window

DN is advertised 3–7 points above MM. Its extra revenue over MM is funding
accruing on the inventory that market-making leaves behind — the same funding
that netted 0.19 % over the year. In the model DN and MM came out identical to
four significant figures, and that is not a bug: with flat funding there is
nothing for the premium to come from.

## 3. For MM and DN, the economics are not the constraint — our own flow is

Tier A cannot produce an MM headline, and the reason is worth more than a number
would have been.

Measured addressable flow — taker volume that actually crossed a band at each
half-spread, and the gross spread a maker capturing **all** of it would earn:

| half-spread | taker flow crossing it | gross spread if we took ALL |
|---|---|---|
| 10 bp | $30.4 bn | $30.4 M |
| 15 bp | $21.2 bn | $31.7 M |
| 25 bp | $11.4 bn | $28.4 M |
| 40 bp | $5.5 bn | $21.9 M |
| 60 bp | $2.7 bn | $16.0 M |

Inverted, the question becomes answerable — what share must the vault win?

| half-spread | for 12 % APY | for 18 % APY | for 25 % APY |
|---|---|---|---|
| 10 bp | 0.039 % | 0.059 % | 0.082 % |
| 15 bp | 0.038 % | 0.057 % | 0.079 % |
| 25 bp | 0.042 % | 0.063 % | 0.088 % |
| 40 bp | 0.055 % | 0.082 % | 0.114 % |
| 60 bp | 0.075 % | 0.112 % | 0.156 % |

Four parts in ten thousand. **The spread economics are not what stands between
us and 12–18 %.**

**But that flow is Binance's, not ours.** The number says the edge exists in the
market; it says nothing about whether our order book will see it. That is the
liquidity question already parked in `docs/trading-track-open-questions.*.md`,
and this analysis sharpens it rather than answering it: the MM band is a
*volume* question, not a *strategy* question.

## 4. Method note — the first model was wrong in the flattering direction

The first implementation produced a headline: **383 % APY, 0 % drawdown**. Three
things gave it away — revenue scaled exactly linearly with the assumed quote
size, DN matched MM to four digits, and the drawdown was zero across every
parameter set.

The cause: our quote is capped on **100 %** of the minutes where price crosses
our band, because median taker flow in such a minute is ~65,000 XRP against the
~150 XRP we would show. So the "model" was `size × edge × count(minutes)` — an
arithmetic identity returning its own input. It also never booked inventory
mark-to-market, which is why it could only produce profit.

That model is not in the tool. What shipped reports what the data settles
without a fill model, and says plainly where a fill model is required (Tier B).

## 5. Caveats

- **Binance is the proxy for both flow and funding.** Our venue computes its own
  funding from its own book; a thin book gives noisier, not systematically
  larger, funding. Our `compute_funding_rate` clamps at ±5 bp per 8h — in this
  window **0.0 %** of periods would have hit the clamp, so the clamp is not what
  suppressed the carry.
- **Delta One models infrastructure that does not exist** (spot RLUSD/XRP,
  lending). The borrow proxy is a reference rate, not a quote we could get
  against XRP collateral on XRPL today.
- **Maker rebate is 0 bp** throughout, because that is what the venue has
  committed (taker 5 bp, maker not rebated). A 1 bp rebate moves the required
  capture share by roughly a tenth of itself — it does not change any conclusion.
- **No adverse selection is modelled anywhere.** Every number here is therefore
  an upper bound on the MM/DN side.
